# Instagram Layout System

## Standard canvases
- Feed default: 1080 × 1350 px (4:5).
- Story/Reel cover: 1080 × 1920 px (9:16).
- Square: 1080 × 1080 px (1:1).
- Carousel: 3–7 slides, each 1080 × 1350 px unless requested otherwise.

## Layout
- One clear headline and one supporting message.
- Keep text, logo and CTA inside safe areas; use generous margins (about 7% of canvas width as a starting point).
- Do not place essential text on busy hair, eyes, hands or high-detail backgrounds.
- Keep contrast strong enough for mobile viewing.
- Use a single focal point; avoid overloading the poster with app feature lists.

## Compositing
Generate photography/background first, then add exact typography, official logo and genuine app screenshot as separate layers. Do not rely on image generation to spell product names, render text, or invent a believable app UI.

## Carousel consistency
Keep headline alignment, typography scale, color system, logo placement and footer treatment consistent. Each slide should have one purpose and readable copy.
