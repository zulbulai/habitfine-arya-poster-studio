# Negative Prompt Library

Tailor the negative prompt to the scene. Useful baseline:

“different person, changed facial identity, wrong eye color, different hair color, straight loose hair when bun is specified, plastic skin, excessive beauty retouching, unrealistic anatomy, distorted hands, extra fingers, fused fingers, duplicated limbs, asymmetrical eyes, warped face, unnatural joints, floating objects, inconsistent shadows, oversaturated skin, low-resolution face, blurry subject, fake text, misspelled words, logos, watermarks, fabricated app UI, fake ratings, fake download counts, fake testimonials, fake prices, fake scarcity, cluttered composition, text over face, text over hands.”

Do not include constraints that contradict the requested scene. Avoid sexualized framing for general fitness/lifestyle advertising.
