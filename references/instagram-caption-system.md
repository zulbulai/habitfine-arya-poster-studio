# Instagram Caption System — HabitFine × Arya

## Default deliverable
Unless the user requests a narrower output, every campaign includes:
1. Ready-to-publish caption matching the poster.
2. Alternative opening hook.
3. Relevant hashtags (usually 5–10; no hashtag stuffing).
4. Optional first comment.
5. Accessibility alt text describing the actual final creative.
6. Publication checks for features, pricing, offers and store CTA.

## Caption structure
- **Hook:** One specific opening line; avoid repeating the poster headline verbatim unless intentional.
- **Context:** Explain a relatable daily friction or moment.
- **Product connection:** Mention only features marked CONFIRMED in `app-detail.md`.
- **Action:** One accurate CTA; never invent a URL, store badge, price, offer expiry or eligibility rule.
- **Hashtags:** Relevant brand, topic and audience tags; never promise reach or virality.
- **Alt text:** Describe only visible subject, scene, on-image text, logo and screenshot actually present.

## Voice
Use natural Hinglish when appropriate for an Indian audience. Keep lines short, mobile-readable and encouraging. Avoid shaming. Emojis are optional and restrained. Arya may speak in first person only as an explicitly fictional brand voice, never as a real customer testimonial.

## Claim safety
Never invent features, current price, ratings, download counts, testimonials, awards, user statistics or scarcity. Avoid guaranteed health/productivity outcomes. Do not imply medical benefits. Do not claim step tracking, hydration measurement, diet tracking, wearable integration, cloud sync or AI coaching unless confirmed. Use “Download on Google Play” only when current listing and CTA are verified. “Free” requires checking current purchase terms. Exclude `TRYYEAR` unless its current terms are validated.
