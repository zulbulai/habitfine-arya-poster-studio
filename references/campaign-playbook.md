# Campaign Playbook

## Campaign types
1. Brand awareness — everyday routines, relatable moments, neutral brand-level copy.
2. Confirmed feature spotlight — only a feature marked CONFIRMED in `app-detail.md`, ideally paired with a genuine app screenshot.
3. Routine inspiration — lifestyle motivation without implying unverified product functionality.
4. Validated promotion — only with current confirmed pricing, eligibility, redemption and terms.
5. Carousel education — one idea per slide, clear progression, final slide with a verified CTA.

## Concept workflow
For each brief define objective, audience, one message, visual metaphor, Arya scene, headline, supporting copy, CTA, format, caption and QA.

## Sample angles
- Morning reset: Arya reading/journaling by a window.
- Everyday movement: Arya outdoors in warm natural light.
- Fitness consistency: Arya training in the gym.
- Calm start: Arya stretching on a balcony.
- Work-life routine: Arya at a cafe with a laptop.

These are scene ideas, not claims about HabitFine features. Verify any product-specific language before use.
