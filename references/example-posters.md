# Example Posters & QA Test Cases

These examples are creative concepts, not evidence of current app functionality. Any product CTA must be checked against current app status.

## Test 1 — Brand awareness
- Objective: Awareness
- Headline: “Small Steps. Daily.”
- Supporting line: “Make room for one manageable action today.”
- CTA: “Explore HabitFine” (verify that the destination/CTA is appropriate)
- Scene: Arya in a calm morning setting with clean negative space.

## Test 2 — Habit tracking spotlight (BLOCKED until confirmed)
- Headline: “Make Your Habits Visible”
- Supporting line: “Track the routine you choose to practice.”
- Precondition: Habit tracking must be CONFIRMED and genuine app UI supplied.

## Test 3 — Streak spotlight (BLOCKED until confirmed)
- Headline: “Show Up Again Today”
- Supporting line: “A small action is still progress.”
- Precondition: Do not promise a specific streak duration or outcome.

## Test 4 — Focus timer (BLOCKED until confirmed)
- Headline: “Give One Task Your Attention”
- Supporting line: “Make space for a focused work session.”
- Precondition: Confirm the focus timer exists before naming it or showing its UI.

## Rejected examples
- “The Routine App You Won’t Quit.”
- “Guaranteed results in 30 days.”
- “Your brain needs the checkmark dopamine.”
- Any fabricated rating, testimonial, download count, price or scarcity claim.

## QA
- Claims have CONFIRMED evidence.
- Official logo is the supplied image, not AI-redrawn.
- Only genuine app screenshots/UI are used.
- Typography is composited accurately and readable.
- Arya matches approved references.
- CTA is current and accurate.
- Caption matches the final visual.
