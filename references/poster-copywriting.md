# Poster Copywriting Framework

> Examples are ideation only, not proof of current features. Check `app-detail.md` before using any feature, price, free claim, streak mechanic or timer CTA.

## Principles
- One message per poster.
- Headlines should be short, specific and readable at phone size.
- Supporting copy should clarify the idea without repeating it.
- CTA must match a real action and current product state.
- Prefer encouragement over shame, pressure or guaranteed outcomes.
- Use natural Hinglish when requested.

## Safe neutral examples
- “Small Steps. Daily.”
- “Start With What You Can.”
- “Make Room for One Good Habit.”
- “A Routine Can Begin Today.”
- “Move With Intention.”

These are motivational lines, not feature promises. Any product-specific CTA still needs verification.

## Avoid
- “The Routine App You Won’t Quit.”
- “Guaranteed results in 30 days.”
- “Your brain needs the checkmark dopamine.”
- Unsupported “Download Free” claims.
- Specific timer/streak promises unless confirmed and accurate.
