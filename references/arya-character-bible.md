# Arya Character Bible

Arya is a fictional AI-generated adult Indian fitness and lifestyle creator, age 22 in the project brief.

## Identity anchors
- Warm tan skin with natural texture.
- Hazel-brown eyes.
- Dark espresso-brown hair in a textured messy bun, with soft loose strands around temples and face.
- Athletic, fit but believable proportions; realistic anatomy and natural posture.
- Minimal delicate gold necklace with three small charms when visible.
- Natural makeup, expressive but grounded facial expression.

## Reference priority
Use `assets/arya-portrait-golden-hour.png` as the primary face anchor; pair with `arya-front-neutral.png`, `arya-profile-neutral.png`, and `arya-full-body-turnaround.png` for identity and proportions. Attach the real reference images to any generation tool that supports reference conditioning. Without reference conditioning, do not promise exact facial identity.

## Wardrobe and scenes
Choose scene-appropriate, premium athletic or lifestyle clothing. The beige set is a reference outfit, not a mandatory outfit for every campaign. Use the actual scene assets only as visual references; they do not establish product features.

## Consistency checklist
- Same adult character and facial structure.
- Similar eye color, hairline, bun and loose strands.
- Natural hands, joints, skin texture and body proportions.
- Avoid sudden changes to age, skin tone, hair color or body type.
- Avoid over-retouching, plastic skin and exaggerated anatomy.
