# App Feature & Claims Policy

## Authority order
1. `app-detail.md` with evidence and status.
2. Current app build screenshots and behavior.
3. Current official Google Play listing.
4. Dated owner confirmation recorded in `app-detail.md`.
5. Old project copy — discovery leads only, never proof.

## Claim statuses
- CONFIRMED: current evidence exists.
- UNVERIFIED: evidence missing or stale; do not state as fact publicly.
- HISTORICAL: may no longer be current.
- PLANNED: not released; never market as available.
- NOT SUPPORTED: omit.

## Process
For every proposed claim, identify the precise statement, check its status, phrase confirmed claims narrowly, and remove or query unverified ones. Never use a mockup or generated UI as evidence.

## Specific risks
Old notes mention daily checkoffs, streaks, progress overviews, habit categories, focus timer, ambient sounds, XP/badges, share cards, heatmap, breathing exercises, backup and premium tiers. Their appearance in old documents does not prove current availability.

Do not claim automatic 10,000-step tracking, hydration measurement, nutrition tracking, wearable sync, cloud sync, AI coaching, real-money penalties or guaranteed outcomes without direct evidence.

## Pricing, offers and social proof
Never assume historical pricing remains current. Do not say “free” without checking current purchase terms. `TRYYEAR` is user-provided and unverified; use only after current redemption and terms are documented. Never fabricate expiry, ratings, reviews, download counts, testimonials, awards, rankings or scarcity.
