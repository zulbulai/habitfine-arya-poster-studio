# HabitFine Brand Guide

## Brand identity
Product: HabitFine: Daily Habit Tracker. Android package: `com.procsctools.habitfine`. Current product claims must be checked in `app-detail.md`.

## Official logo
Use the exact supplied `assets/HabitFine-app-icon.png` file when available. It shows a friendly blue star-shaped mascot holding a white card with an electric-blue checkmark on a dark rounded-square background. Do not redraw, distort, recolor, crop into an unreadable mark, or ask an image generator to recreate it.

## Creative direction
- Premium, friendly, clean, modern.
- Clear hierarchy and generous whitespace.
- Rounded corners and restrained visual effects.
- Use real app screenshots when showing UI; keep them separate from generated photography.
- Suggested palette from project design notes: deep navy `#1F3352`, terracotta `#C05A2B`, cream `#F4EFE7`; use the actual app icon colors as a brand anchor.
- Typography suggestions: Playfair Display/Canela-style display headings and Inter/Poppins-style body. Use installed/licensed fonts available in the design tool.

## Product-claim boundary
Brand colors, logo descriptions and old campaign copy do not prove that a feature exists. Use `app-detail.md` and `references/app-feature-and-claims-policy.md` for all product claims.
